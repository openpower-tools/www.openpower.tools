Evidence-backed support matrix for software on POWER, with live capability probes of your own browser.

Projects under the openpower.tools umbrella; each lives at /project/{name}/.

  • OpenPOWER Foundation: Power ISA specifications and working groups
  • Raptor Computing Systems wiki: Talos II and Blackbird documentation, firmware, compatibility lists
  • Raptor community forums: operating systems, porting and hardware discussion
  • Talospace: news on OpenPOWER desktops and ports
  • skiboot (OPAL): OpenPOWER boot and runtime firmware
  • op-build: builds Hostboot, Skiboot, OCC and Petitboot into a PNOR image
  • OpenBMC: BMC firmware used on Talos II and Blackbird
  • #talos-workstation: IRC on Libera.Chat, bridged to Matrix
