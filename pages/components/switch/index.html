A native checkbox drawn as a switch, for settings that take effect immediately. The control keeps checkbox semantics for assistive tech. The empty side of the track prints the state as the IEC power numerals - 1 on, 0 off - and hovering slides a ghost of the thumb to the state a click would reach.

Markup

<op-switch>
  <label><input type="checkbox" /> Verbose boot output</label>
</op-switch>
