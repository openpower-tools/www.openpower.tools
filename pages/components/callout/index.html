Highlighted blocks for notes, tips and warnings. Severity is shown three ways at once - the variant word set in the severity colour where a heading would sit, an ISO-shaped icon, and colour - so none of them has to carry the meaning alone. Notes and tips carry a thin stripe and a dimmed watermark of their icon on the right; warnings and danger anchor on a solid stripe at the left and show a dark barberpole in their own colour - at the right for warning, wrapped around for danger - with a scrim under the wording. Content is regular light-DOM markup on a slightly raised background.

Variants

The default variant, for neutral asides and context. For advice and good practice, striped with the ok green. For things that can bite: a left amber stripe, the dark-amber barberpole showing at the right. For destructive operations: a left red stripe and a dark-red barberpole wrap; the wording carries the severity.

Without a heading

A bare callout: just the variant tag and content, for short asides.

Icons

The icon shapes follow the ISO 3864 shape grammar as registered in ISO 7010: a triangle for hazard warnings (W001), a circle with a diagonal bar for prohibition-grade danger (P001), a circled i for information, and the square of the safe-condition series for tips. Notes and tips keep a thin stripe and a dimmed watermark of their icon on the right; warning and danger frames anchor on a left stripe and draw a dark barberpole in their own colour behind a translucent scrim - revealed at the right for warning, wrapped for danger - so the wording stays readable. The hues stay in the matching ISO colour families - amber, red, blue, green - but use the theme's contrast-checked status tokens rather than raw signal colours, which would fail WCAG AA on these backgrounds.

Markup

<op-callout variant="warning" heading="Warning">
  Flashing the wrong PNOR image can brick the BMC.
</op-callout>
