Highlighted blocks for notes, tips and warnings. Severity is shown three ways at once - the variant word set in the severity colour where a heading would sit, an ISO-shaped icon, and colour - so none of them has to carry the meaning alone. Every variant anchors on a colour stripe at the left and carries a dimmed watermark of its icon on the right; warnings and danger add a barberpole mixed from their own colour - dark bands in the dark theme, pale in the light: a faint texture under warning's scrim, visible bands above and below danger's. Content is regular light-DOM markup on a slightly raised background.

Variants

The default variant, for neutral asides and context. For advice and good practice, striped with the ok green. For things that can bite: the triangle on the right, the pole a faint texture behind. For destructive operations: the roundel on the right, barberpole bands above and below; the wording carries the severity.

Without a heading

A bare callout: just the variant tag and content, for short asides.

Icons

The icon shapes follow the ISO 3864 shape grammar as registered in ISO 7010: a triangle for hazard warnings (W001), a circle with a diagonal bar for prohibition-grade danger (P001), a circled i for information, and the square of the safe-condition series for tips. Every frame anchors on a left colour stripe and watermarks its icon on the right; warning and danger add a barberpole mixed from their colour toward the theme's base behind a translucent scrim: a faint texture for warning, visible bands above and below for danger, the wording readable on the scrim. The hues stay in the matching ISO colour families - amber, red, blue, green - but use the theme's contrast-checked status tokens rather than raw signal colours, which would fail WCAG AA on these backgrounds.

Markup

<op-callout variant="warning" heading="Warning">
  Flashing the wrong PNOR image can brick the BMC.
