Highlighted blocks for notes, tips and warnings. The stripe carries the variant colour; text stays on the standard token for contrast. Content is regular light-DOM markup.

Variants

The default variant, for neutral asides and context. For advice and good practice, striped with the ok green. For things that can bite, striped with the warning amber. For destructive operations, striped with the danger red; the wording carries the severity.

Without a heading

A bare callout: just the variant tag and content, for short asides.

Markup

<op-callout variant="warning" heading="Warning">
  Flashing the wrong PNOR image can brick the BMC.
</op-callout>
