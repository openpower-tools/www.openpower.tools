Highlighted blocks for notes, tips and warnings. Severity is shown three ways at once - the variant word, an ISO-shaped icon and a colour - so none of them has to carry the meaning alone. Content is regular light-DOM markup.

Variants

The default variant, for neutral asides and context. For advice and good practice, striped with the ok green. For things that can bite, striped with the warning amber. For destructive operations, striped with the danger red; the wording carries the severity.

Without a heading

A bare callout: just the variant tag and content, for short asides.

Icons

The icon shapes follow the ISO 3864 shape grammar as registered in ISO 7010: a triangle for hazard warnings (W001), a circle with a diagonal bar for prohibition-grade danger (P001), a circled i for information, and the square of the safe-condition series for tips. The hues stay in the matching ISO colour families - amber, red, blue, green - but use the theme's contrast-checked status tokens rather than raw signal colours, which would fail WCAG AA on these backgrounds.

Markup

<op-callout variant="warning" heading="Warning">
  Flashing the wrong PNOR image can brick the BMC.
</op-callout>
