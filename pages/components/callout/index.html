Highlighted blocks for notes, tips and warnings. Severity is shown three ways at once - the variant word set in the severity colour where a heading would sit, an ISO-shaped icon, and colour - so none of them has to carry the meaning alone. The frame is a severity ladder: notes and tips stay quiet, warnings post their amber-bordered triangle on the left, and danger reads like a hazard plate. Content is regular light-DOM markup on a slightly raised background.

Variants

The default variant, for neutral asides and context. For advice and good practice, striped with the ok green. For things that can bite: the amber-bordered triangle posted on the left. For destructive operations: plated in the danger red on both sides, wording centred.

Without a heading

A bare callout: just the variant tag and content, for short asides.

Icons

The icon shapes follow the ISO 3864 shape grammar as registered in ISO 7010: a triangle for hazard warnings (W001), a circle with a diagonal bar for prohibition-grade danger (P001), a circled i for information, and the square of the safe-condition series for tips. The frame spends more ink as the stakes rise: notes and tips keep a thin stripe and a contrasting watermark of their icon on the right; warnings post their triangle on the left, drawn with the warning amber as its border; danger carries the roundel on both sides with the title and message centred between them, like a hazard plate. The hues stay in the matching ISO colour families - amber, red, blue, green - but use the theme's contrast-checked status tokens rather than raw signal colours, which would fail WCAG AA on these backgrounds.
