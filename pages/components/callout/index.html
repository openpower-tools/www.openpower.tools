Highlighted blocks for notes, tips and warnings. Severity is shown three ways at once - the variant word set in the severity colour where a heading would sit, an ISO-shaped icon, and colour - so none of them has to carry the meaning alone. Warning and danger carry their icon on the left, notes and tips on the right - each knocked out of a solid block of its colour - so the severe and the advisory separate at a glance. Content is regular light-DOM markup on a slightly raised background.

Variants

The default variant, for neutral asides and context. For advice and good practice, striped with the ok green. For things that can bite; the left block carries the warning amber. For destructive operations; the left block carries the danger red and the wording carries the severity.

Without a heading

A bare callout: just the variant tag and content, for short asides.

Icons

The icon shapes follow the ISO 3864 shape grammar as registered in ISO 7010: a triangle for hazard warnings (W001), a circle with a diagonal bar for prohibition-grade danger (P001), a circled i for information, and the square of the safe-condition series for tips. Every icon is knocked out of a solid block of its severity colour in the page background colour, like an ISO sign: warning and danger blocks sit on the left, note and tip blocks on the right beside the thin stripe, so the two groups also separate by side. The hues stay in the matching ISO colour families - amber, red, blue, green - but use the theme's contrast-checked status tokens rather than raw signal colours, which would fail WCAG AA on these backgrounds.
