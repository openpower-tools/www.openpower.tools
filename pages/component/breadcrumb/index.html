Where-am-I navigation. The trail is a regular ordered list of links, the last marked with aria-current; the separators are drawn by the stylesheet so they stay out of the accessible name.

  3. Breadcrumb

Markup

<opt-breadcrumb>
  <ol>
    <li><a href="/">Home</a></li>
    <li><a href="/here/" aria-current="page">Here</a></li>
  </ol>
</opt-breadcrumb>
